RESEARCH BACKGROUND

Materials science

1997 · Inorganica Chimica Acta

A new strontium-copper ethylene glycol complex: synthesis and structure of [Sr(C2H6O2)5][Cu(C2H4O2)2]·C2H6O2

Alexander R. Pico; Carol S. Houk; Timothy J.R. Weakley; Catherine J. Page.

Inorganica Chimica Acta 258(2): 155-160 (1997).

A synthesis-and-structure study of a strontium–copper ethylene glycol complex. An early publication connecting chemical preparation with structural characterization.

This paper concerns a chemical complex; it is not presented here as a demonstration of superconductivity.

RESEARCH BACKGROUND

Protein structure and function

2001 · Neuron

Structure of the RCK Domain from the E. coli K+ Channel and Demonstration of Its Presence in the Human BK Channel

Youxing Jiang; Alexander Pico; Martine Cadene; Brian T. Chait; Roderick MacKinnon.

Neuron 29(3): 593-601 (2001).

X-ray crystallography, structure-based sequence alignment, and mutational analysis connect a bacterial potassium-channel regulatory domain to the gating machinery of the human BK channel.

Peng Yuan; Manuel D. Leonetti; Alexander R. Pico; Yichun Hsiung; Roderick MacKinnon.

Science 329(5988): 182-186 (2010).

A structural study of the human BK channel’s calcium-activation apparatus. The work describes a gating ring and calcium-binding sites, linking molecular architecture to channel regulation.

RESEARCH BACKGROUND

Bioinformatics & Software

Open scientific infrastructure, data integration, reproducible analysis, and machine learning.

2008 · PLoS Biology

WikiPathways: pathway editing for the people

Alexander R Pico; Thomas Kelder; Martijn P van Iersel; Kristina Hanspers; Bruce R Conklin; Chris Evelo.

PLoS Biology 6(7): e184 (2008).

Introduces a community-curated approach to biological pathways, combining a wiki model with graphical pathway editing. Connects open scientific infrastructure with a practical way to organize and share biological knowledge.

Read the publication ↗

2010 · BMC Bioinformatics

The BridgeDb framework: standardized access to gene, protein and metabolite identifier mapping services

Martijn P van Iersel; Alexander R Pico; Thomas Kelder; Jianjiong Gao; Isaac Ho; Kristina Hanspers; Bruce R Conklin; Chris T Evelo.

BMC Bioinformatics 11(1): 5 (2010).

Presents a shared interface for mapping gene, protein, and metabolite identifiers across different data services. The framework separates analysis tools from individual mapping providers, supporting data integration and multi-omics workflows.

Read the publication ↗

2019 · Genome Biology

Cytoscape Automation: empowering workflow-based network analysis

David Otasek; John H. Morris; Jorge Bouças; Alexander R. Pico; Barry Demchak.

Genome Biology 20(1): 185 (2019).

Connects interactive network analysis with scripted Python and R workflows through standardized interfaces. A contribution to making scientific analysis more reproducible, scalable, and reusable.

Read the publication ↗

2023 · BMC Genomics

Using published pathway figures in enrichment analysis and machine learning

Min-Gyoung Shin; Alexander R. Pico.

BMC Genomics 24(1): 713 (2023).

Explores how biological pathway figures from the literature can become a resource for enrichment analysis and machine learning. Includes case studies in cancer subtype and grade prediction, connecting scientific diagrams with computational analysis.
