Questions for October 2026

Each month a deterministic, local pipeline looks at the projects I am working on in public (essays, research series, later newsletters and data products) and picks up to three threads. The score combines my editorial priority, the project’s stage, how fresh its field records are, tags not featured recently, and a penalty for repeating last month. Then one question is written per thread: the thread’s guiding question picks the angle, and a fixed phrasing is filled only with field records from the research record, named by short titles, and the thread’s tags. My background and publications never steer either step. No language model or network service writes these questions.
