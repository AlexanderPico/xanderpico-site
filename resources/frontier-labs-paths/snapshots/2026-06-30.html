B. Steering through conditions on chips and contracts

80%range 65–90%+2 pts vs simulated 2026-03-31

Steering through conditions on chips and contracts. The state governs frontier compute it does not own by attaching conditions to export licenses and investment agreements.[183][185] It is path G applied abroad first, and it is already running: Microsoft–G42 operates under an "Intergovernmental Assurance Agreement," and the 2025 G42 and HUMAIN approvals carry "security and reporting requirements."[183][185]

C. Frontier labs as users and partners of national facilities

53%range 38–68%−2 pts vs simulated 2026-03-31

Frontier labs as users and partners of national facilities. AI follows the beamline pattern: government-owned compute (Solstice, Lux, Doudna) allocated by merit review, with company work handled as proprietary use at full cost recovery, CRADAs or Strategic Partnership Projects.[134][158] This is the path most grounded in how Argonne and Berkeley Lab already work.

Resolves YES if

E. A public lab spins out a company

22%range 10–38%+1 pts vs simulated 2026-03-31

A public lab spins out a company. A government lab builds a capability and hands it to a new firm, as ITRI did with TSMC in 1987.[196] ITRI had earlier licensed IC technology from RCA in 1976 and spun off UMC in 1980.[196][197] The flow runs opposite to the thesis, from public to private.

Method and re-scoring rules (from the latest snapshot)
independence
Each probability is a separate estimate that its path resolves YES by the horizon. Paths can co-occur, so probabilities do not sum to 1; when one path's resolution makes another more or less likely, say so in the rationale rather than normalizing.
signpost mapping
Each signpost has a direction. not_yet->emerging moves the path roughly 0.03-0.05 in that direction; emerging->happened or not_yet->happened roughly 0.05-0.15, depending on how close the signpost is to the resolution criterion. A reversal (happened back to not_yet) moves it by the same amount the other way. Keep low<=point<=high and widen the range when evidence conflicts.
resolution
A path resolves YES when its resolution_criteria are met by a public document dated after 2026-10-22 and on or before the horizon; it resolves NO at the horizon otherwise. Record the resolving ledger id. A resolved path keeps probability 1 or 0 in later snapshots.
cadence
Re-score quarterly, and also within two weeks of any event that changes a signpost to 'happened'.

Why this horizon: Spans the next presidential term and a full contracting or export-licensing cycle, close enough that the 2026 record still bears on it, and matches stated delivery dates such as FugakuNEXT's 'around 2030' (ledger 177).
