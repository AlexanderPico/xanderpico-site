# Frontier Labs as National Laboratories

Nine possible paths for how the US government and frontier AI labs might relate, each with signposts and an estimated probability of happening by 2030. Re-scored over time; every snapshot is kept. The first measurement is 2026-10-22; the three earlier quarter-end points (March, June, September 2026) are simulated retrospective estimates, drawn as hollow dots, not contemporaneous measurements. Paths are ordered by current estimate.

Snapshot 2026-11-12 · horizon **by end of 2030** (2030-12-31)

Each probability is a separate estimate that its path happens by the horizon. Paths can co-occur, so they do not sum to 100%. Estimates: draft estimates by the research team.

## Paths

### A. Ad hoc levers continue (the status quo)

90%range 80–96%+5 pts

Ad hoc levers continue (the status quo). Testing stays voluntary, controls are applied model by model, and disputes go to court.

#### Resolves YES if

Resolves YES if between 2026-10-23 and 2030-12-31 the federal government applies at least two new firm- or model-specific measures to named frontier developers or named makers of advanced AI chips: an export control or license decision on named models or chips, a procurement designation, a negotiated pre-release access agreement, or a firm-specific enforcement action.

### B. Steering through conditions on chips and contracts

82%range 62–92%+2 pts

Steering through conditions on chips and contracts. The state governs frontier compute it does not own by attaching conditions to export licenses and investment agreements.[[183]](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2024/04/15/microsoft-and-g42-partner-to-accelerate-ai-innovation-in-uae-and-beyond)[[185]](https://www.commerce.gov/news/press-releases/2025/11/statement-uae-and-saudi-chip-exports) It is path G applied abroad first, and it is already running: Microsoft–G42 operates under an "Intergovernmental Assurance Agreement," and the 2025 G42 and HUMAIN approvals carry "security and reporting requirements."[[183]](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2024/04/15/microsoft-and-g42-partner-to-accelerate-ai-innovation-in-uae-and-beyond)[[185]](https://www.commerce.gov/news/press-releases/2025/11/statement-uae-and-saudi-chip-exports)

#### Resolves YES if

Resolves YES if between 2026-10-23 and 2030-12-31 there are at least three new publicly announced US export approvals, licenses or government-backed agreements for foreign AI compute that state security, reporting, know-your-customer or monitoring conditions, or a federal statute or published federal rule that imposes such conditions, including chip tracking or location verification.

### C. Frontier labs as users and partners of national facilities

60%range 45–75%±0 pts

Frontier labs as users and partners of national facilities. AI follows the beamline pattern: government-owned compute (Solstice, Lux, Doudna) allocated by merit review, with company work handled as proprietary use at full cost recovery, CRADAs or Strategic Partnership Projects.[[134]](https://science.osti.gov/User-Facilities/Frequently-Asked-Questions)[[140]](https://doeleadershipcomputing.org/call-for-proposals)[[158]](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/TTWG_Partnering_Guide.pdf) This is the path most grounded in how Argonne and Berkeley Lab already work.

#### Resolves YES if

Resolves YES if between 2026-10-23 and 2030-12-31 a named frontier developer is publicly documented training, fine-tuning or running frontier-model research on DOE-owned compute under a user agreement, CRADA, Strategic Partnership Project or merit allocation.

#### Signposts

- Not yet↑ an INCITE-style call that allocates frontier-scale AI compute as of 2026-10-22
- Emerging↑ Lux-type public-private co-investments repeated as of 2026-10-22
  - [DOE — New public-private partnership model, two supercomputers (Lux and Discovery at ORNL, Oct 27, 2025)](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-new-public-private-partnership-model-two-supercomputers) [144]
- Emerging↑ Genesis awards moving from credits to company-led frontier-model work as of 2026-10-22
  - [DOE — First Genesis Mission projects selected (278 awards, Jul 22, 2026)](https://www.energy.gov/articles/secretary-energy-chris-wright-announces-first-genesis-mission-projects-selected-accelerate) [147]
- Emerging↓ user-facility openness 'without regard to nationality' colliding with access restrictions on frontier models as of 2026-10-22

### D. A national-champion bargain

35%range 20–52%+5 pts

A national-champion bargain. A Bell-style arrangement: protection, procurement and possibly equity in exchange for a public return.[[101]](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/pol.20190086)[[109]](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/trump-open-ai-altman-stake.html)

#### Resolves YES if

Resolves YES if by 2030-12-31 the US government acquires equity, warrants, a golden share or a contractual revenue share in a frontier developer or a maker of advanced AI chips, or signs an agreement granting it preferred status explicitly in exchange for a public return. Stakes taken before 2026-10-23, including the 2025 Intel stake, do not count.

#### Signposts

- Emerging↑ a finalized government equity stake, and whether it carries governance rights as of 2026-11-12
  The 2025 Intel stake is finalized and carries no governance rights. It predates the resolution window, so it is a precedent, not a resolving event. No stake in a frontier developer has been finalized.

  - [Semafor: Trump advisers weigh structure of potential AI stakes (Jun 17, 2026)](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/17/2026/trump-advisers-weigh-structure-of-potential-ai-stakes) [110]

### E. A public lab spins out a company

25%range 12–40%±0 pts

A public lab spins out a company. A government lab builds a capability and hands it to a new firm, as ITRI did with TSMC in 1987.[[196]](https://50th.itri.org.tw/en/history/semiconductors/2) ITRI had earlier licensed IC technology from RCA in 1976 and spun off UMC in 1980.[[196]](https://50th.itri.org.tw/en/history/semiconductors/2)[[197]](https://itritoday.itri.org/114/content/en/unit_01-2.html) The flow runs opposite to the thesis, from public to private.

#### Resolves YES if

Resolves YES if by 2030-12-31 a model trained at a DOE national laboratory, or the core team that built it, moves to a new company or nonprofit under a license, CRADA or similar agreement that is publicly announced.

#### Signposts

- Not yet↑ a DOE science model such as AuroraGPT, or its team, moving into a company or nonprofit as of 2026-10-22
  - [ALCF — Argonne releases Aurora exascale supercomputer to researchers (Jan 28, 2025)](https://www.alcf.anl.gov/news/argonne-releases-aurora-exascale-supercomputer-researchers) [141]
- Not yet↑ Genesis platform tools licensed out under CRADA terms as of 2026-10-22
  - [DOE — First Genesis Mission projects selected (278 awards, Jul 22, 2026)](https://www.energy.gov/articles/secretary-energy-chris-wright-announces-first-genesis-mission-projects-selected-accelerate) [147]
  - [DOE Technology Transfer Working Group — Partnering Guide (CRADA, SPP; Dec 2024)](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-12/TTWG_Partnering_Guide.pdf) [158]
- Not yet↑ an explicit DOE spin-out policy for lab-trained models as of 2026-10-22
- Emerging↓ frontier developers supplying the models the labs use as of 2026-10-22
  - [DOE — First Genesis Mission projects selected (278 awards, Jul 22, 2026)](https://www.energy.gov/articles/secretary-energy-chris-wright-announces-first-genesis-mission-projects-selected-accelerate) [147]

### F. An AI FFRDC under an M&O contract

The government owns a secure AI facility and contracts an operator, possibly an LLC of companies and universities on the Triad or UT-Battelle pattern, with performance ratings and recompetes as the accountability tool.[[148]](https://www.energy.gov/documents/nnsa-2018pdf)[[149]](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/2018/06/new-22b-los-alamos-management-contract-awarded/347794)[[152]](https://www.battelle.org/laboratory-management)

#### Resolves YES if

### G. The regulated circuit

15%range 7–30%±0 pts

The regulated circuit. Compute and model access are licensed, covering both training runs and distillation through APIs.[[125]](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks)[[126]](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a)

#### Resolves YES if

Resolves YES if by 2030-12-31 a federal statute or final rule requires licensing, registration with approval, or mandatory pre-release access for frontier models or large AI data centers, or imposes know-your-customer duties on compute or API customers.

#### Signposts

  Chip tracking by sellers, as in the proposed Chip Security Act, is counted under conditions on chips and contracts, not here. This path still needs a duty on compute or API providers.

Method and re-scoring rules (from the latest snapshot)

independence

Each probability is a separate estimate that its path resolves YES by the horizon. Paths can co-occur, so probabilities do not sum to 1; when one path's resolution makes another more or less likely, say so in the rationale rather than normalizing.

signpost mapping

Each signpost has a direction. not_yet->emerging moves the path roughly 0.03-0.05 in that direction; emerging->happened or not_yet->happened roughly 0.05-0.15, depending on how close the signpost is to the resolution criterion. A reversal (happened back to not_yet) moves it by the same amount the other way. Keep low<=point<=high and widen the range when evidence conflicts.

resolution

A path resolves YES when its resolution_criteria are met by a public document dated after 2026-10-22 and on or before the horizon; it resolves NO at the horizon otherwise. Record the resolving ledger id. A resolved path keeps probability 1 or 0 in later snapshots.

cadence

Re-score quarterly, and also within two weeks of any event that changes a signpost to 'happened'.

**Why this horizon:** Spans the next presidential term and a full contracting or export-licensing cycle, close enough that the 2026 record still bears on it, and matches stated delivery dates such as FugakuNEXT's 'around 2030' (ledger 177).
