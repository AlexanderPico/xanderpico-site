AI-native biotech observatory

A dated snapshot of 41 companies, plus a growing ledger of what a few of them said they would do and what they say later. It is meant to be refreshed and compared week to week.

How to read this. The plot places companies using unreviewed machine readings of verbatim interview quotes; it is a sorting aid, not a measurement. Ledger quotes come from company filings, are matched word for word against the saved filing text, and each is linked. Plans are compared with later statements, not with trial results. A statement not found by phrase search is not a retraction. Research and commentary, not investment advice.
