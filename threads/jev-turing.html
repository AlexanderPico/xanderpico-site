Jev Turing

What can be learned by treating one probabilistic, typed AI judgment as a logic primitive and checking each result against a known Boolean rule?

An independent experiment that uses a Jev Boolean judgment as the only logic primitive for gates, an SR latch, and Rule 110 cell updates, with recorded runs and a ground-truth checker. It is not affiliated with TypeSafe AI or Vercel.

  2. 2004

    Jev Turing · Paper · Complex Systems 15, 1–40

    Universality in Elementary Cellular Automata

    Cook's paper gives a proof that Rule 110 is capable of universal computation and states the binary nearest-neighbor update rule used by the experiment's ground-truth checker.

    Source ↗ Jev Turing

  3. 1983-07-01

    Jev Turing · Paper · Reviews of Modern Physics 55, 601

    Statistical mechanics of cellular automata

    Wolfram's paper analyzes elementary cellular automata with binary sites that update from nearest neighbors in discrete time, the rule family from which Rule 110 is drawn.

    Source ↗ Jev Turing

  4. 1956

    Jev Turing · Paper · Automata Studies, Princeton University Press, pp. 329–378

    Probabilistic logics and the synthesis of reliable organisms from unreliable components

    von Neumann treats error as an essential part of automata synthesis and frames networks as connected basic components with finite time delays. It is the source the project cites for making an explicit error budget and for distinguishing a noisy voter from the gate it votes over.

    Source ↗ Jev Turing
