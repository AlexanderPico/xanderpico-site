# AI-native biology, beyond the announcements

**Guiding question:** What are AI-native biotech companies betting on, how do their approaches differ, and what changes as the work meets reality?

A research series on the companies rebuilding biological discovery around AI: what they are betting on, how their approaches differ, and what changes as the work meets reality.

## Key findings

- Four AI-first drug discovery companies (BigHat, Genesis, Absci, Manas AI) name accuracy in molecule design as the scarce thing while owning very different labs; Recursion's executive names target choice instead. Source: [Same shortage, different labs](https://xanderpico.com/resources/ai-native-lab-ownership/index.md)
- Of 15 Recursion statements scored against the company's later statements, 7 were marked met, mostly milestones the company largely controls. Source: [Recursion: what it said it would do, and what it says now](https://xanderpico.com/resources/recursion-plans-vs-later/index.md)
- Recursion's CEO said in 2024 that its pipeline would have 100 programs; its FY2025 annual report says up to 40 small molecule programs over a decade or longer. Source: [Recursion promised 100 drugs, then proof](https://xanderpico.com/resources/recursion-what-it-adds-up-to/index.md)
- Recursion said 13 of 15 evaluable patients had a deep reduction in polyp burden after 12 weeks on REC-4881; the page notes no described comparison group. Source: [Recursion promised 100 drugs, then proof](https://xanderpico.com/resources/recursion-what-it-adds-up-to/index.md)
- Recursion, Relay, Schrödinger and Absci have each moved at least one program toward a partner or a spin-out this year or last, as of the Q3 2026 comparison. Source: [Four AI-first drug companies, one quarter](https://xanderpico.com/resources/ai-native-q3-2026-comparison/index.md)
- Among the speed and efficiency claims in the four companies' Q3 2026 documents, one names its comparison: Recursion's 204 compounds against about 2,500. Source: [Four AI-first drug companies, one quarter](https://xanderpico.com/resources/ai-native-q3-2026-comparison/index.md)
- Relay's annual reports moved its stated cash runway from into 2025 to into 2029, while the cash figure in the same sentence went from $998.9 million to $554.5 million. Source: [When a company moves its own date: observatory issue 2](https://xanderpico.com/resources/ai-native-observatory-issue-2/index.md)

Limits: Entries are machine-selected and not reviewed by a person, and plans are compared with each company's later statements, not with trial results; the pages are research and commentary, not investment advice.

## Records on this thread

- 2025-06-03: [A generative AI-discovered TNIK inhibitor for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis: a randomized phase 2a trial](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-025-03743-2). Randomized, placebo-controlled phase 2a trial of rentosertib, a TNIK inhibitor described as AI-generated, in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
- 2024-11-20: [Recursion completes business combination with Exscientia](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1601830/000160183024000217/exhibit993-closingprexai_r.htm). Two AI-driven drug discovery companies combined, with Exscientia becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Recursion.
- 2024-05-08: [Accurate structure prediction of biomolecular interactions with AlphaFold 3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07487-w). Predicts joint structures of complexes of proteins, nucleic acids, small molecules, ions and modified residues.
- 2023-07-11: [De novo design of protein structure and function with RFdiffusion](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8). A general diffusion-model framework for de novo protein design, including binder design and higher-order symmetric architectures.
- 2021-11: [Isomorphic Labs announced as a new Alphabet company](https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles/introducing-isomorphic-labs). A DeepMind spin-out set up to "reimagine the entire drug discovery process from first principles with an AI-first approach", building on AlphaFold2.
- 2021-07-15: [Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2). AlphaFold predicts protein structures with high accuracy from sequence.
