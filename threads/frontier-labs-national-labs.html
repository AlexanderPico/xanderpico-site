As national security and international competition press on the frontier AI labs, which past model of a national laboratory do they come to resemble, and where does the comparison break down?

An essay on whether national security, secure methods and international competition will push the frontier AI labs toward national-laboratory status, and what past labs suggest about the shape that could take.
