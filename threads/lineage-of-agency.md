# The wave and the leash

**Guiding question:** Who should count the failures when people hand more of what they do to AI, and how can anyone tell that the counting is independent of the thing being counted?

An essay on the long line of capacities people have handed to tools and institutions, why most of us will not refuse the next handoff to AI, and who should independently count its failures. Includes Who counts?, a quarterly ledger of how AI is being checked.

## Resources

- Essay, 2026-11-16: [The wave and the leash](https://xanderpico.com/resources/lineage-of-agency/index.md) ([web page](https://xanderpico.com/resources/lineage-of-agency/index.html)). People have always handed parts of their lives to things bigger and faster than any one of us. AI may be the first handoff to reach the whole loop of sensing, remembering, learning, deciding and acting. Most people will not refuse it, so the practical question is who counts its failures, independently of the thing counted.
- Tracker, 2026-11-16: [Who counts? A ledger of how AI is checked](https://xanderpico.com/resources/who-counts/index.md) ([web page](https://xanderpico.com/resources/who-counts/index.html)). For each standard, law, company framework, research method, tribunal ruling and insurance product that checks AI: who writes the definitions, who pays the checker, when the authority ends, whose evidence is used and what the checker loses if wrong. Quotations and links only, no grades. Refreshed quarterly.

## Records on this thread

- 2026-09-17: [Underwriting Superintelligence: Backing Agents you can Sue — Rune Kvist, AIUC](https://latent.space/p/aiuc/). Rune Kvist, CEO of the Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company, said that for houses, cars and nuclear power the market ran ahead of regulation to create confidence infrastructure, and described AIUC-1 as a standard for AI agents backed by insurance.
- 2026-09-09: [California AB 1405: AI auditor registration](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billVersionsCompareClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB1405). California enacted a law creating a registry of AI auditors under which a violation is grounds for removal from the registry; the governor approved it on 9 September 2026.
- 2026-07-06: [Illinois SB 315 (Public Act 104-0538)](https://ilga.gov/Legislation/PublicActs/PrinterFriendly/104-0538). Illinois enacted a law requiring a large frontier AI developer to retain a third party each year to perform an independent audit of compliance; the governor approved it on 6 July 2026 and it takes effect on 1 January 2027.
- 2026-02-18: [Measuring AI agent autonomy in practice](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy). Anthropic analyzed millions of human-agent interactions in Claude Code and its public API and reported that experienced Claude Code users auto-approve more frequently but also interrupt more often.
- 2024-02-14: [Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149](https://www.canlii.org/en/bc/bccrt/doc/2024/2024bccrt149/2024bccrt149.html). A British Columbia tribunal held Air Canada responsible for what its website chatbot told a customer about bereavement fares and ordered it to pay a total of $812.02.
- 2023-12-12: [AI Control: Improving Safety Despite Intentional Subversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942). Greenblatt, Shlegeris, Sachan and Roger tested protocols for using a powerful untrusted model with a weaker trusted model, including trusted editing and untrusted monitoring, against strategies the untrusted model could use to subvert them.
- 2018-05-02: [AI safety via debate](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00899). Irving, Christiano and Amodei proposed training agents through a debate game in which two agents make statements and a human judges which gave the most true, useful information, for tasks too complicated for a human to judge directly.
- 2017-12: [Dude, you broke the future!](https://www.antipope.org/charlie/blog-static/2018/01/dude-you-broke-the-future.html). In a keynote at the 34th Chaos Communication Congress, Charles Stross called corporations very old, very slow AIs and asked what their history says about the likely behavior of the artificial intelligence being built now.
