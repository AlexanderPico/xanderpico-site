    How AI and Human Behaviors Shape Psychosocial Effects of Extended Chatbot Use: A Longitudinal Randomized Controlled Study

    A four-week randomized experiment (n=981) of text and voice chatbot modes: the assigned conditions had no significant effects, but participants who used the chatbot more showed consistently worse outcomes on loneliness, social interaction, emotional dependence and problematic use.
