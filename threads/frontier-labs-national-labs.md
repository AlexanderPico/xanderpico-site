# Frontier labs as national laboratories

**Guiding question:** As national security and international competition press on the frontier AI labs, which past model of a national laboratory do they come to resemble, and where does the comparison break down?

An essay on whether national security, secure methods and international competition will push the frontier AI labs toward national-laboratory status, and what past labs suggest about the shape that could take.

## Key findings

- Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq on November 19, 2026. Its final prospectus shows no government equity, golden share or special government right, and government agencies were under 2% of revenue in every period shown. Source: [Anthropic goes public... to stay private.](https://xanderpico.com/resources/anthropic-goes-public/index.md)
- As of the 2026-11-19 snapshot, the highest estimate is 92% (range 72–97%) that ad hoc levers continue, the status quo, through 2030. Source: [Frontier labs paths tracker](https://xanderpico.com/resources/frontier-labs-paths/snapshots/2026-11-19.html)
- Steering through conditions on chips and contracts resolved YES on November 19, 2026, when a Commerce rule imposing reporting and know-your-customer conditions on chips for foreign data centers was published; recorded in the 2026-11-20 snapshot. Source: [Frontier labs paths tracker](https://xanderpico.com/resources/frontier-labs-paths/snapshots/2026-11-20.html)
- Frontier labs as users and partners of national facilities is estimated at 60% (range 34–75%) by 2030, as of the 2026-11-19 snapshot. Source: [Frontier labs paths tracker](https://xanderpico.com/resources/frontier-labs-paths/snapshots/2026-11-19.html)
- A national-champion bargain, with protection and procurement in exchange for a public return, is estimated at 35% (range 20–52%) by 2030, as of the 2026-11-19 snapshot. Source: [Frontier labs paths tracker](https://xanderpico.com/resources/frontier-labs-paths/snapshots/2026-11-19.html)
- An AI FFRDC under an M&O contract, where the government owns a secure AI facility and contracts an operator, is estimated at 20% (range 8–35%), as of the 2026-11-19 snapshot. Source: [Frontier labs paths tracker](https://xanderpico.com/resources/frontier-labs-paths/snapshots/2026-11-19.html)
- A national laboratory is a government-owned facility run by a contractor the government can grade and replace; the Department of Energy has 17, with 16 under management-and-operating contracts. Source: [Frontier AI labs are already treated like national labs. Just not the way that counts.](https://xanderpico.com/resources/frontier-labs-newsletter/index.md)

Limits: The probabilities are judgment-based estimates that can co-occur and do not sum to 100%; the March, June and September 2026 points are simulated retrospective estimates, not measurements made at the time.

## Records on this thread

- 2026-09-29: [White House Accord on Super Intelligence: Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/white-house-accord-super-intelligence). A voluntary accord signed by the President and six frontier AI companies asks each developer for four layers of controls and audits, including an independent external auditor and an independent board committee; the text says it may make sense to codify these steps.
- 2026-07-22: [Secretary of Energy Chris Wright Announces First Genesis Mission Projects Selected to Accelerate AI-Driven Scientific Discovery](https://www.energy.gov/articles/secretary-energy-chris-wright-announces-first-genesis-mission-projects-selected-accelerate). After merit review, DOE announced 278 awards: 87 led by DOE and NNSA national laboratories, 168 by universities, 19 by companies and 4 by nonprofits.
- 2025-11-24: [Launching the Genesis Mission](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/11/launching-the-genesis-mission). Executive Order 14363 launches a national AI-for-science effort, compares its urgency and ambition to the Manhattan Project, and directs DOE to run an AI platform that meets national-security requirements.
- 2025-10-28: [Energy Department Announces New Partnership with NVIDIA and Oracle to Build Largest DOE AI Supercomputer](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-new-partnership-nvidia-and-oracle-build-largest-doe-ai). DOE announced Solstice (100,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs, the largest AI supercomputer in the DOE lab complex) and Equinox (10,000 GPUs) at Argonne, built with NVIDIA and Oracle.
- 2025-08-21: [Developing nuclear safeguards for AI through public-private partnership](https://www.anthropic.com/news/developing-nuclear-safeguards-for-ai-through-public-private-partnership). Anthropic, NNSA and DOE national laboratories co-developed a classifier that separates concerning from benign nuclear-related conversations with 96% accuracy in preliminary testing; Anthropic reports it is deployed on Claude traffic.
- 2024-07-16: [DOE Announces Roadmap for New Initiative for Artificial Intelligence in Science, Security and Technology](https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-announces-roadmap-new-initiative-artificial-intelligence-science-security-and). DOE's roadmap for FASST, an initiative in which DOE and its 17 national laboratories aim to build integrated scientific AI systems for science, energy and national security with academic and industry partners.
